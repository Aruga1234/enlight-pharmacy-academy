Featured Program

Graduate Pharmacy Aptitude Test (GPAT) Training

V-Enlight Pharmacy Academy offers GPAT-oriented training and academic preparation designed to help pharmacy students systematically prepare for the Graduate Pharmacy Aptitude Test.

The program focuses on strengthening fundamental pharmaceutical knowledge, improving problem-solving ability, developing examination strategies and providing structured practice.

Who Can Join?

  • B.Pharm students
  • Final-year B.Pharm students
  • B.Pharm graduates
  • Students preparing for postgraduate pharmacy entrance examinations
  • Candidates seeking structured GPAT preparation
